# "The New Car" (15-second clip, 3D) — Life as a GTA 6 NPC, Ep. 3

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical for TikTok/Shorts, 30 fps, 450 frames, with audio. Blender/Cycles, the same look, street and NPC as Eps. 1–2.
**Logline:** The NPC has just bought his first new car after three years of saving. He owns it for about four seconds before the player takes it. He doesn't let go of the door handle.
**Characters:** the NPC (Hawaiian shirt, sunglasses, iced coffee) · his new car (a low, wide, bright yellow supercar: black bonnet stripe, big rear wing, gold wheels, a big red gift bow on the roof) · the player (pink hoodie, hood up, seen only from behind, never a face)
**Comedy style:** Deadpan and polite. He's being dragged down the street like a flag and he's still saying "excuse me". He never lets go of his coffee.
**Cartoon rule:** Nobody gets hurt. He flaps in the wind, lands neatly on a bench, and is fine.
**Hook (frame 1):** Cold open on the best moment: the yellow supercar speeding down the street with the NPC hanging off the door handle by one hand, his whole body streaming up and back like a flag, coffee held perfectly level in his other hand. Then rewind.

**Beat:** hook (dragged like a flag, sipping coffee) → rewind → "Three years of saving. Worth it." → the player takes the car → he hangs on, politely → flung onto the bench → the bow lands on his head: "Three more years." → title.

**Why it should work:**
- Frame 1 is a strong, strange image (a man flying like a flag off a car door, calmly drinking coffee) and it's a new shape for the series: sideways and fast, not thrown through the air.
- Everyone who has played an open-world crime game has done this to an NPC; this is that moment from his side.
- Tight setup and payoff: "Three years of saving" → "Three more years", with the bow as the last touch.
- Reuses the street, the NPC and the car rig, so it reads as a series and is quick to build.

**New to build:** the yellow supercar with a gift bow (a new, original design), the player in a pink hoodie (the existing figure, hood up, back to camera), a bench.
