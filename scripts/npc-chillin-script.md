**Tone:** Deadpan cartoon slapstick. The NPC is never surprised. This happens to him every day. All hits are cartoon physics: he flattens like a pancake and pops back, with no blood or injuries shown.
**Hook (frame 1):** The NPC spinning mid-air over the pink sports car, police lights flashing below, totally calm: "Ah. Tuesday."

**Beat:** hook (calm mid-air "Ah. Tuesday.") → running gag (lamppost) → peace (bench) → threat (5-star chase approaching) → his own AI betrays him (runs into the road) → launch over the car → lands → five police cars run him over → peels himself off the road → seagull steals the coffee → swear.

**Why it's funny:**
- He's calm while flying through the air. The joke is that this is normal for him.
- The cops are chasing the pink car but run over the innocent NPC five times anyway, and nobody stops.
- The five "Ow"s are the rule of three stretched to five: each one flatter and more tired, so the last "...ow" gets the biggest laugh.
- The gags come from game bugs people recognize: bad pathfinding, NPCs running the wrong way, T-poses, ragdoll physics.
- The only swear is the last line, so it lands as the punchline.
- The seagull wins.
