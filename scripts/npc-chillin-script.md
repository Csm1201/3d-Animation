**Beat:** hook (mid-air freeze "OH SH—") → flashback context (peak chill) → threat (engine rev, denial) → impact (the launch) → payoff (trash can, "respawn me").

**Comedy notes:**
- The cold open shows him flying first, so the viewer spends the calm part waiting for it to happen.
- His "sidewalk is safe" line is the setup and the car mounting the curb is the punchline.
- The hit stays cartoon: a bonk, a spin and a trash-can landing. No blood, no injuries shown.
- Each bleep runs a beat too long. That's funnier than a tight edit.

**Vertical cut (9:16):** Crop centered on the NPC. In the launch shot, the camera tilt follows him up, which suits a tall frame. Keep captions above the bottom 20%.
