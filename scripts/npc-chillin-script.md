# "Just Chillin'" (15-second clip)

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical for TikTok/Shorts (plus a 1920×1080 cut), 30 fps, 450 frames, with audio
**Logline:** A background NPC's one perfect moment of peace is ruined by the player, his own bad AI, and the police.
**Setting:** Sunset beach-city street. Pastel buildings, neon signs, palm trees, a bench, a lamppost, sidewalk above a two-lane road.
**Characters:** Pedestrian #4812 (Hawaiian shirt, sunglasses, iced coffee). A seagull. The player (seen only as a pink sports car). Two cops.
**Tone:** Deadpan. The NPC is never surprised. This has happened to him thousands of times.
**Hook (frame 1):** The NPC spinning mid-air above the street, coffee flying, totally calm: "Ah. Tuesday."

**Beat:** hook (calm mid-air "Ah. Tuesday.") → running gag (lamppost bonks) → peace (bench) → threat (engine) → his own AI betrays him (runs into the road) → launch → glitch landing → he's the one arrested → seagull steals the coffee → swear.

**Why it's funny:**
- He's calm while flying through the air. The joke is that this is normal for him.
- Each gag comes from game bugs people recognize: bad pathfinding, NPCs running the wrong way, T-poses, clipping into the ground, cops arresting the wrong person.
- The only swear is the last line, so it lands as the punchline instead of being noise.
- The seagull wins.
