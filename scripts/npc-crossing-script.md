# "The Crossing" (14-second clip, 3D) — Life as a GTA 6 NPC, Ep. 3

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical for TikTok/Shorts, 30 fps, 420 frames, with audio. Blender/Cycles, the same look, street and NPC as Eps. 1–2.
**Logline:** The NPC refuses to cross on red. A full five-star chase tears through the crossing around him, and he doesn't move a muscle. The light finally turns green, he steps out, and a runaway shopping trolley takes him out.
**Characters:** the NPC (Hawaiian shirt, sunglasses, iced coffee) · the pink sports car (the player) · two police cars · the police helicopter · one empty shopping trolley
**Comedy style:** Deadpan. Total chaos around him and he's completely calm; the only thing that gets him is the slowest object in the episode. He never lets go of his coffee.
**Cartoon rule:** Nobody gets hurt. Cars miss him by centimetres; the trolley knocks him flat and he's fine.
**Hook (frame 1):** Cold open on the best moment: a police car upside down in mid-air, directly over his head in slow motion, while he stands at the red light sipping his coffee without looking up. Then rewind.

**Beat:** hook (police car flipping over his head, he doesn't flinch) → rewind → "Never cross on red." → the whole chase misses him → green man, "Finally." → a shopping trolley takes him out → "Should've crossed on red." → title.

**Why it should work:**
- Frame 1 is absurd on its own (an upside-down police car over a calm man sipping coffee) and different from the airborne-NPC hooks of Eps. 1–2: this time he's the still point and the chaos flies.
- Classic setup and payoff: survives five stars, loses to a trolley. The last line flips his first line.
- Plays on a familiar game-world joke (NPCs obeying traffic rules in the middle of total chaos) without using any real game's assets.
- Reuses the street, NPC, pink car, police cars and helicopter, so it's quick to build and reads as a series.

**New to build:** zebra crossing stripes, a crossing signal with a push button and red/green man, a shopping trolley.
