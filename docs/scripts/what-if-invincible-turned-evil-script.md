# One-off Short: "What if Invincible turned evil?" (script, draft 5: serious, real world)

About 25 s, vertical, and it stands on its own. This is the serious version, set in **our real world**. There are no other superheroes, only governments, militaries and everyday people, plus his dad.

The hook of the idea: if someone that powerful turned evil here, what would he take out first? He goes after the things that could hurt him or organise against him, in a cold, logical order. Each step gets a title card.

**Violence:** dramatic but not gory. He destroys **hardware, not people**. Satellites, missile silos and an evacuated aircraft carrier are shown from far away. There is no blood, no injuries and no deaths on screen. Impacts read through shockwaves, dust, sparks and twisted metal.

**Characters (parody, in our style, pushed moodier):**
- **Invincible / Mark:** classic suit (yellow top with blue side panels, blue sleeves, gloves, trousers and goggles). When evil, his eyes glow red and a red rim light falls on him.
- **Omni-Man:** white suit, red cape, black moustache, grey at the temples.
- **Everyday people:** generic crowds and soldiers, seen small and far away. They have no lines; they react.
- No other heroes. In the real world there's nobody else.
