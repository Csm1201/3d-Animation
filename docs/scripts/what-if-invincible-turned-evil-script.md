# One-off Short: "What if Invincible turned evil?" (script, draft 3)

About 22 s, vertical, and it stands on its own, in the chunky 3D toy style of *You're the Ref*.

It's a parody of **Invincible** (Mark Grayson). The joke is that when he turns evil, it's incredibly petty. He has god-tier powers and spends them on the most annoying everyday crimes, until one phone call ends it.

**Invincible, toy-style:** a teen with dark hair in his classic suit: a yellow top with blue panels down the sides, blue sleeves, blue gloves, blue trousers and boots, and blue goggles. He has no cape. Evil mode: his eyes glow red behind the goggles and a red rim light falls over him, with no other costume change.

**Characters:**
- **Invincible:** hero turned smug, petty villain, with one line and an evil laugh.
- **Office worker, car-park shopper, plane passenger:** generic citizens with no lines, only reactions.
- **Mom:** heard only, as a voice on the phone (never shown), with one line.
- **Narrator:** a big dramatic trailer voice for the title only.

## Voices

All voices are generic stock voices, not imitations of anyone:
- **Narrator:** a deep dramatic trailer voice, for the title line only.
- **Invincible:** a young, bright stock voice, never an imitation of the show's actor. One line ("Mine.") and the evil laugh.
- **Mom:** a calm, warm adult stock voice, slightly phone-filtered, not an imitation of the show's actor. One line: "Mark. Did you leave a trolley out?"
