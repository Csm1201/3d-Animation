# One-off Short: "What if Invincible turned evil?" (script, draft 6: real world vs the army)

About 21 s, vertical, and it stands on its own. This is the serious version, set in **our real world**, with no other heroes. There's no backstory: it starts the moment he begins wrecking everything. Then it's **the army against him**, escalating step by step, and nothing works.

**Violence:** big and destructive, but not gory. He wrecks **vehicles, weapons and buildings, not people**. Crews always bail out, eject or swim clear before or as their vehicle goes. No blood, injuries or deaths are shown. Impacts read through shockwaves, smoke, sparks, twisted metal and flying debris.

**Characters (parody, in our style, pushed moodier and more real-world):**
- **Invincible / Mark:** classic suit (yellow top with blue side panels, blue sleeves, gloves, trousers and goggles). His eyes glow red the whole time, with a red rim light.
- **The army:** generic soldiers, tank crews, helicopter and jet pilots, and generals. No real flags, insignia, country names or real leaders.
- **Civilians:** generic crowds, running.
- No other heroes and no Omni-Man. In the real world, it's just us against him.
