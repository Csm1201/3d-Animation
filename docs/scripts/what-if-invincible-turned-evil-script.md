# One-off Short: "What if Invincible turned evil?" (script, draft 4: serious)

About 24 s, vertical, and it stands on its own. This is the **serious, dramatic version**: no petty jokes. It plays it straight, like a dark "what if" episode of the show. It uses our 3D style, pushed moodier with night and dawn light, smoke, dust and broken glass.

**The story:** Mark finally decides his dad was right. He stops being the hero, overpowers everyone who tries to stop him, and in the end turns on his father too.

**Violence:** dramatic but not gory. Impacts read through shockwaves, dust, glass and buildings cracking. There is no blood, no injuries shown and no deaths on screen. People are knocked away, never killed.

**Characters (parody, in our style):**
- **Invincible / Mark:** classic suit (yellow top with blue side panels, blue sleeves, gloves, trousers and goggles). When evil, his eyes glow red and a red rim light falls on him.
- **Omni-Man:** white suit, red cape, black moustache, grey at the temples.
- **Atom Eve:** pink suit and red hair, with a pink energy glow.
- **The heroes:** four generic caped silhouettes, not specific Guardians, so we don't have to design a whole team.
- **Police:** generic, with no lines.
