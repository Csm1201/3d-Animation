# The NPC, Episode 1: "Launch Day" (script, draft 3)

About 30 s, vertical, in the same toy-style 3D as *You're the Ref*. It loops: the last frame cuts back into the hook.

**Dale** is a background pedestrian in a pastel, neon Florida beach town. He wears a short-sleeve shirt and a lanyard, and is clutching a pint of milk. His whole purpose in the game is one errand: getting a pint of milk home from the shop. He has never once made it. He is completely used to this: no panic, no surprise, just a tired man who knows exactly how his day is going to go.

This is the story campaign, with no other players. Dale's day is wrecked by the game's **main character**: a sunburnt guy in a loud shirt and sunglasses who spends the whole episode on one long police chase. Every character, the city and the cars are original. There are no GTA characters, no logos and no copied interface art (no wanted stars or minimap). "GTA 6" appears only in the title, description and hashtags, as parody and commentary.

## Voices

All voices are stock vidIQ voices, not imitations of anyone:
- **Dale:** a bright, upbeat stock voice (Charlie or Chris, to audition).
- **The main character:** screams only, no lines.
- **Police radio:** one line, filtered through a radio effect.
- **No narrator.** The rewind stamp and on-screen text do that job.
- **Dale's delivery:** deadpan and weary throughout, like a commuter whose train is late again. He never screams; the swears are muttered, not shouted.
- **Swearing:** Captions show the swears uncensored, with the swear word highlighted in red, as in *You're the Ref*.
