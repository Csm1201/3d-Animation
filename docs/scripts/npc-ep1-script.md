# The NPC, Episode 1: "Five. Always Five." (script, draft 4)

About 18 s, vertical, in the same toy-style 3D as *You're the Ref*. It loops: the last frame cuts back into the hook.

**Dale** is a background pedestrian in a pastel, neon Florida beach town. He wears a short-sleeve shirt and a lanyard, and is clutching a pint of milk. His whole purpose in the game is one errand: getting a pint of milk home from the shop. He has never once made it. He is completely used to this: no panic, no surprise, just a tired man who knows exactly how his day is going to go.

This is the story campaign, with no other players. Dale's day is wrecked by the game's **main character**: a sunburnt guy in a loud shirt and sunglasses, in a hot-pink sports car, on a police chase. This episode is one gag only: the police cars running him over. Every character, the city and the cars are original. There are no GTA characters, no logos and no copied interface art (no wanted stars or minimap). "GTA 6" appears only in the title, description and hashtags, as parody and commentary.

## Voices

All voices are stock vidIQ voices, not imitations of anyone:
- **Dale:** a bright stock voice played tired and flat (Charlie or Chris, to audition).
- **The officer:** one line, a different stock voice.
- **The main character:** screams only, no lines.
- **No narrator.** The rewind stamp and on-screen text do that job.
- **Dale's delivery:** deadpan and weary throughout, like a commuter whose train is late again. He never screams; the swears are muttered, not shouted.
- **Swearing:** captions show the swears uncensored, with the swear word highlighted in red, as in *You're the Ref*.

- **Respawn montage:** a downdraft into a fountain, a goose, a jet ski. "Not the fucking goose—"

- **Delivery:** Dale gives up and orders the milk online. The delivery driver is the main character.
