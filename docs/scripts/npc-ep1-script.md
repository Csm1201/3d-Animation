# The NPC, Episode 1: "Launch Day" (script, draft 1)

About 30 s, vertical, in the same toy-style 3D as *You're the Ref*. It loops: the last frame cuts back into the hook.

**Dale** is a background pedestrian in a pastel, neon Florida beach town. He wears a short-sleeve shirt and a lanyard, and always has an iced coffee in his hand. He has exactly one line of dialogue, and he's very proud of it: "Beautiful day in Palm Vista!" Today the game launches, and millions of players log in at once.

Players appear as blank-faced avatars with a floating gamertag above their heads, like `xX_D3STROY3R_Xx`. Every character, the city and the cars are original. There are no GTA characters, no logos and no copied interface art. "GTA 6" appears only in the title, description and hashtags, as parody and commentary.

## Voices

All voices are stock vidIQ voices, not imitations of anyone:
- Dale: a bright, upbeat stock voice (Charlie or Chris, to audition).
- Players: silent. Their personality comes from gamertags and body language.
- No narrator. The rewind stamp and on-screen text do that job.
