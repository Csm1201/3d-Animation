# One-off Short: "What if Mega Boy turned evil?" (script, draft 1)

About 22 s, vertical, and it stands on its own, in the chunky 3D toy style of *You're the Ref*.

The format is "what if [superhero] turned evil", with an **original hero, Mega Boy**, not any existing character. The joke is that his "evil" is incredibly petty. He has god-tier powers and spends them on the most annoying everyday crimes, until Jimmy the ref turns up.

**Mega Boy's design (original, not based on any existing hero):** a teen in a bright **green and white** suit with a big lightning-bolt "M" on the chest, a short green cape, white gloves and boots, and spiky orange hair. He wears no mask and has no goggles. When he's evil, his eyes glow red, a red shadow falls over him, and his cape flaps dramatically even indoors.

**Characters:**
- **Mega Boy:** cheerful hero turned smug, petty villain, with two short lines.
- **Office worker, car-park shopper, plane passenger:** generic citizens with no lines, only reactions.
- **Jimmy the ref:** deadpan, with one line.
- **Narrator:** a big dramatic trailer voice for the title only.

## Voices

All voices are generic stock voices, not imitations of anyone:
- **Narrator:** a deep dramatic trailer voice, for the title line only.
- **Mega Boy:** a young, bright stock voice, a different one from Jimmy's. Two lines: "Mine." and the evil laugh.
- **Jimmy:** George, deadpan. One line: "Put the trolley back."

## Production notes

- **New character:** Mega Boy, as designed above. Green, white and orange are kept deliberately away from existing heroes' colour schemes. The lightning "M" emblem and the cape are new rig attachments.
- **New effects:**
  - red glowing eyes (emissive) and a red rim light while he's evil;
  - a flying pose with the cape flapping;
  - a whoosh speed trail;
  - a Mega-Boy-shaped ceiling hole.
- **New sets:**
  - a supermarket car park at dusk with lightning, a trolley and a trolley bay;
  - an office party table;
  - a plane cabin (two rows of seats);
  - a city street with billboards.
- **Props:**
  - a shopping trolley;
  - pizza slice and box;
  - a car (simple boxy toy car);
  - phones and billboards showing the "RE: RE: RE: lunch??" text;
  - Jimmy's two red cards.
- **Reused:**
  - Jimmy's rig and the red card;
  - the A vs B ending panels and the comment button;
  - the record scratch, whistle and stamp sounds.
- **Copyright:** no existing hero's name, costume, emblem or colours. If the title says a real character's name, the video must not show that character.
- **Self-review checks:**
  - the hook trolley push matches the payoff;
  - the caption is a question;
  - Mega Boy looks clearly original;
  - every line is clear over the thunder.
