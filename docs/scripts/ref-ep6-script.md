# You're the Ref, Episode 6: "Jimmy Gets a Ladder" (script, draft 3)

About 35 s, vertical. Continues straight from Episode 5: LeBron kicked the keeper (and the ball) into the top corner, and Jimmy gave the goal. Scoreboard: BLU 1-1 ORA, PENS, with shootout dots (Blue 1 scored, Orange 1 scored).

## How South Park would write it

1. **"But" and "therefore", never "and then".** Every beat causes the next one, or blocks it.
2. **A tiny problem escalates into an institution.** A red card that's too low turns into health-and-safety paperwork, then a live TV special. Nobody ever says "this is mad".
3. **Everyone is dead serious.** The steward is a real jobsworth, the TV presenter is solemn, and Jimmy treats a ladder like procedure. The comedy comes from the gravity.
4. **Long awkward silence.** Hold a beat until it's uncomfortable, then hold it longer.
5. **Satirise the celebrity, not the person.** LeBron gets his own "The Decision"-style live special over a red card. He never speaks, so it's all pompous TV graphics and silence.
6. **The sincere speech that misses the point, cut off by consequences.** "You know, I learned something today..." then CLANG.
7. **Hard, crude button.** End on one flat swear, not a joke explained.
8. **Cartoon logic.** Injuries never carry over. The keeper is never mentioned again.

## Characters

- **Jimmy:** deadpan, procedural, never surprised.
- **LeBron:** no lines, played through size, silence and body language. He has no scenes with Epstein, and the invite is not shown in this episode.
- **"Mogs":** said once only.

## Answer for Episode 7

**No goal, and the penalty is retaken.** The stepladder is an outside object on the pitch. Under Law 9, if the ball touches an outside object, play stops. For a penalty kick that means a retake. Jimmy agrees, then gives it anyway. The steward confiscates the ladder.
