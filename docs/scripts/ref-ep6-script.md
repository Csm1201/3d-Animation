# You're the Ref, Episode 6: "Jimmy Gets a Ladder" (script, draft 2)

About 33 s, vertical. Continues straight from Episode 5: LeBron kicked the keeper (and the ball) into the top corner, and Jimmy gave the goal. Scoreboard: BLU 1-1 ORA, PENS, with shootout dots (Blue 1 scored, Orange 1 scored).

**Writing rules for this one** (borrowed from how South Park is written):
- **"But" and "therefore", never "and then".** Every beat causes the next one, or blocks it.
- **Absurd problem, dead-serious response.** Nobody treats anything as silly. Jimmy treats a stepladder like procedure.
- **One real-world target:** stadium health-and-safety jobsworths.
- **The sincere speech that gets cut off.** Jimmy tries to say what he "learned today" and gets interrupted.
- **Cartoon logic.** Injuries never carry over. Nobody mentions the keeper again.

**Characters:**
- **Jimmy:** deadpan as always.
- **LeBron:** no lines, played through size and body language. No scenes with Epstein, and the invite is not shown in this episode.
- **"Mogs":** said once only.

## Answer for Episode 7

**No goal, and the penalty is retaken.** The stepladder is an outside object on the pitch. Under Law 9, if the ball touches an outside object, play stops. For a penalty kick that means a retake. Jimmy agrees, then gives it anyway.
