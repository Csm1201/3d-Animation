# You're the Ref, Episode 6: "Jimmy Gets a Ladder" (script, draft 5)

About 31 s, vertical. Continues straight from Episode 5: LeBron kicked the keeper (and the ball) into the top corner, and Jimmy gave the goal. Scoreboard at the start: BLU 1-1 ORA, PENS.

The story in two halves:
1. **LeBron:** Jimmy sends him off. That's the last we see of LeBron.
2. **The shootout:** three hours later it's 67-67, nobody has missed, and Jimmy has had enough.

**Characters:**
- **Jimmy:** deadpan as always, getting more exhausted.
- **LeBron:** no lines, played through size and body language. He has no scenes with Epstein, and the invite is not shown in this episode.
- **"Mogs":** said once only.

## Answer for Episode 7

**No goal, and the kick is retaken by Orange.** The referee isn't a player, so his kick doesn't count for anything: not a goal and not a miss. The Orange player has to retake it. Jimmy agrees, then gives it anyway.
