# You're the Ref, Short: "Jimmy's Worst Call Ever" (script, draft 1)

About 14 s, vertical. A one-gag Short between episodes. It stands on its own with no recap, so a viewer who has never seen the series still gets it. The match is ordinary daytime Blue vs Orange, with the score at 0-0.

**The gag:** Orange score the goal of the season. Jimmy checks VAR, decides the ball "looked at him", and sends off the ball.

**Characters:**
- **Jimmy:** deadpan and completely sure of himself.
- **Orange striker (#9):** scores the screamer, and his celebration dies.
- **Players and crowd:** stare in disbelief.
- No LeBron and no Epstein. "Mogs" is not used.

## Fake comments

- "the ball DID look at him though 😭"
- "B. this man needs to be stopped"
- "red card to a BALL 💀"
- "A. respect the ref"
- "he had the second ball ready... he planned this"

## Voices

All voices are generic stock voices, not imitations of anyone:
- **Narrator:** Daniel.
- **Jimmy:** George, deadpan, with one line ("It looked at me").
