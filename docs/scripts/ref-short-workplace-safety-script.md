# You're the Ref, Short: "Workplace Safety with Jimmy" (script, draft 1)

About 25 s, vertical, and it stands on its own. It is styled like a cheesy company safety video. Jimmy works five different jobs, **always in his full referee uniform** (yellow shirt, black shorts, whistle, cards). He referees each workplace as if it were a match, and every job ends in a silly cartoon death.

Deaths are slapstick only: squash-and-stretch, soot, a halo, or a little gravestone. There is no blood and no injury detail. After each death Jimmy is simply back, deadpan, in the next lesson.

**Characters:**
- **Jimmy:** deadpan, never learns, referees everything.
- **Crane operator:** generic, seen in his cab, no lines.
- **The gang:** four cartoon 1920s-style mobsters in pinstripe suits and fedoras, deliberately old-timey and over the top so they don't look like any real-world group. They have no lines; they only stare.
- **Narrator:** a calm, upbeat corporate safety-video voice.
- No LeBron and no Epstein. "Mogs" is not used.

## Voices

All voices are generic stock voices, not imitations of anyone:
- **Narrator:** Daniel, in a bright corporate tone.
- **Jimmy:** George, deadpan. He has four lines: "Dangerous play.", "Hands where I can see them.", "Handball." and "Offside."
