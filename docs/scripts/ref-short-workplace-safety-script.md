# You're the Ref, Short: "Workplace Safety with Jimmy" (script, draft 1)

About 22 s, vertical, and it stands on its own. It is styled like a cheesy company safety video. Jimmy works four different jobs, **always in his full referee uniform** (yellow shirt, black shorts, whistle, cards). He referees each workplace as if it were a match, and every job ends in a silly cartoon death.

Deaths are slapstick only: squash-and-stretch, soot, a halo, or a little gravestone. There is no blood and no injury detail. After each death Jimmy is simply back, deadpan, in the next lesson.

**Characters:**
- **Jimmy:** deadpan, never learns, referees everything.
- **Crane operator:** generic, seen in his cab, no lines.
- **Narrator:** a calm, upbeat corporate safety-video voice.
- No LeBron and no Epstein. "Mogs" is not used.
