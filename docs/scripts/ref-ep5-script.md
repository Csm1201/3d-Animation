Bonus laws for the Episode 6 breakdown: the goal can't count, for three reasons:
- LeBron wasn't on the pitch when the match ended, so he can't take a kick.
- Kicking the keeper is a red card.
- The ball was in the keeper's hands when it crossed the line, which makes it a foul, not a goal.
