# One-off Short: "What if a really gay guy spawned in the Taliban?" (script, draft 1)

About 16 s, vertical, in the **low-poly reel style**: faceted people, bright sun, a soft background blur, an italic serif caption with a yellow keyword, a counter top-left and a yellow pill (see `renders/lowpoly-style-example.mp4`).

**The angle:** he's the hero and he wins every scene. Within a week he has completely made over a Taliban camp, and they love it. The joke is on the Taliban, never on him being gay, and nobody is hurt.

**Guardrails:**
- No religion: no mosque, prayer, holy book or religious phrases. The jokes are about the group, their camp and their image, not about Muslims or Afghans.
- No violence and no weapons on screen.
- He's never mocked or punished for being gay. He's the coolest, most competent person there.
- Voices are generic stock voices, and his voice isn't a caricature: he's confident and witty, with no lisp gag.

**Characters:**
- **Him:** a crop top, a rainbow bandana, white sneakers and sunglasses on his head. Totally unbothered.
- **The Taliban:** four generic low-poly men with long beards, black turbans and dusty clothes. They start out glaring and end up as his biggest fans. The leader gets one line at the end.
