# Niche research: 3D toy-style animated shorts

Researched November 2026 with vidIQ (YouTube, TikTok and Instagram breakout videos, YouTube search data) and a web search. These are small samples, so treat them as direction rather than proof.

## Who already uses 3D animated shorts

| Niche | Example accounts | Popularity | Crowding |
|---|---|---|---|
| "How it works" / weird facts | Zack D. Films (29.7M subs; +500K subs and 3.2B views in 30 days) and copycats (@ronaldo.d..films4, @3d.films4, @mechorion) | Huge | Very crowded. Avoid. |
| Psychology with 3D skeletons | @theelitemindsett (2.5M views), @humanx_signals (1.1M) | High | Crowded, filling with AI copies |
| Body / medical 3D | @_jarvis.in_ (3M), @medicalscope, @amazing.facts498 (1.1M) | High | Crowded; mistakes cost credibility |
| Cute 3D animals in skits | Capybara (15.6M), otters (2.8M), hamster (2.1M), panda (914K) | Huge | Saturated, cheap to make with AI |
| Office humour animation | jaylen_animations (8.2K subs, 280K–624K views per Short), Animahoney (corporate jargon skit 621K), G Plus Animation (712K subs) | Popular | Moderate, and almost all 2D comedy, not 3D explainers |
| A 3D character explaining one topic | @noahcne (personified pimple, 1.3M views on 1.1K followers) | One breakout | Shows one character teaching one topic can break out |

**Gap:** office life is popular but covered with 2D jokes, and psychology is popular but covered with dark skeleton videos. Nobody found does workplace explainers with a recurring 3D toy character in a bright pastel style.

## Search demand (monthly YouTube searches; competition 0–100, lower is easier)

- office politics: ~47K, competition 39
  - "why smart people lose at office politics": 13.7K, 37
  - "why lazy employees win at office politics": 4.4K, 12
  - "can you design a workplace without office politics": 8K, 28
- standing desk: 36K, rising 57%
- ergonomic desk setup: 11K, rising 58%, competition 39
- AI job interview: 111K, rising 257%, competition 67 (hot but contested)
- first job and payslip topics: under 750 each (low competition, low search; relies on the Shorts feed)

## Niche options for this character

1. **Office psychology, explained** (top pick): why meetings drain you, why the lazy coworker gets promoted.
2. **Your body at a desk**: what 8 hours of sitting does, why your neck hurts at 3pm.
3. **First job, decoded**: payslips, corporate jargon, first-week survival.

Run them as series on one channel with the same character. Test 10–15 Shorts and compare how many viewers stay past the first seconds before committing.
