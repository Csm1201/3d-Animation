# 3D Animation project rules

## Always open with the hook

Every video, clip, script, storyboard, and animatic in this repo starts with its hook: the most surprising, funny, or visually striking moment, shown first.

- **The first frame already shows the action.** No title cards, logos, establishing shots, or slow walk-ins before the hook.
- **The hook lands within 1 second** for clips under 15 seconds, and within 3 seconds for anything longer.
- **Context comes after the hook.** Explain who, where, and why once the viewer is already watching.
- **For loops and short clips, it's fine to start mid-action** (in medias res) and let the ending set up the start again.
- **Check before hand-off:** if the first frame were the thumbnail, would someone stop scrolling? If not, re-cut so it would.

Every script and shot list should mark the hook explicitly (e.g. a `Hook` row at 0.00).

## Review the polished version yourself before hand-off

After a polished (final-quality) render, don't hand it off straight away. Watch it through several times yourself and fix what you find without waiting to be asked.

- **At least three review passes**, each with a different focus:
  1. **Story and timing:** does the hook land, does every beat read, do the jokes have room, does the dialogue sync?
  2. **Every object in every shot:** is each character, vehicle and prop fully in frame and doing what the script says? Look for things cropped at the edge (e.g. only a helicopter's rotor showing), clipping through each other, popping in or out, sinking into the ground, or facing the wrong way.
  3. **Look and finish:** lighting, color, readability on a phone screen, captions, title, audio levels and sync.
- **How to review:** pull frames from the finished file (contact sheets, plus single frames at each cut and at every scripted action) and play the audio against the picture. Don't review from memory of the code.
- **Fix, then re-check.** Re-render the affected frames and repeat the passes until one finds nothing worth changing.
- **Report what you changed** when you hand off, and list anything you chose not to fix, with the reason (for example, render time).
