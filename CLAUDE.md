# Project rules

## Every video opens with a hook

- **Show the payoff first.** The first 1–1.5 s of every video, whether an episode, a Short or a one-off, must be the hook. That means the most surprising, funny or shocking moment of the video, shown first, before any setup, rewind or titles.
- **Make the hook something you see.** Open on a big action, a strange image or a "wait, what?" moment, mid-motion from frame 0. There's no fade-in, logo or slow establishing shot. Sound hits on frame 0 too (a bass hit, a crowd roar or a line of dialogue).
- **No caption that describes the hook.** Viewers can see what's happening. Text over the hook can only be a question or a tease, never a description.
- **Leave it unresolved.** The hook raises a question that only the rest of the video answers, then cuts straight into the story ("rewind", a flashback stamp, or the setup).
- **Check every new script.** Each script in `docs/scripts/` starts with a hook row, and every render is checked against it before delivery.

## "You're the Ref" series

- Never put real people in scenes with Epstein.
- Don't overuse catchphrases: "MOGGED" appears once at most.
- Voices are generic stock voices, never imitations of real people.
